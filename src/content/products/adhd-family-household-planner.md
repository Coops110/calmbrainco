Running a household with ADHD rarely comes down to one hard day. It's the weight of being the only person who remembers the permission slip, the pediatrician's number, whose turn it is to do the dishes, and what's for dinner on Thursday, all at once, all in one head. The ADHD Family and Household Planner is a printable system built around sharing that weight instead of just carrying it better.

Ten pages: a family week-at-a-glance command page built around the brand's usual morning, afternoon and evening blocks so anyone in the household can see where everyone needs to be, a mental load dump that gives the invisible, unshareable parts of running a household somewhere to land, kid routine cards for morning and bedtime sequences written once and reused every day, a household chore rotation that reassigns who does what each week instead of a fixed chart, a school paperwork tracker written as "due in" instead of a calendar date, a contact and info sheet for school, doctor and sitter numbers in one place, caregiver handoff notes for whoever else needs to know what's going on this week, a family meal rotation of repeatable, kid-tolerated meals, and a one thing for me page: a single, deliberately small line for the parent, not a full page.

Pairs naturally with the [ADHD Home Reset Planner](/shop/adhd-home-reset-planner/), which handles cleaning a space: zones, a deep clean rotation, a laundry loop. This planner's Household Chore Rotation is about the people in that space instead, who does what this week. It also pairs with the [ADHD Planner Bundle](/shop/adhd-planner-bundle/)'s [Meal Planner page](/shop/adhd-planner-bundle/#p9): plan the week there, then pull from this planner's Family Meal Rotation on the nights there's nothing left to plan.
