## A budget system built for how ADHD brains actually handle money, not against it

Most budgeting tools assume a brain that checks in daily, categorizes every receipt, and never forgets a bill is due. This one doesn't. The ADHD-Friendly Budget Planner is a printable system built around realistic effort instead of perfect tracking — not another finance app that gets deleted by February.

### One glance, not twelve categories

The monthly overview splits money into three honest buckets — income, fixed expenses, variable expenses — and does the subtraction for you on paper. No spreadsheet, no formulas to get wrong.

### A subscription page that exists because you forgot

Half of what's draining an account is something signed up for months ago and never cancelled. The subscription tracker is one page built specifically to make that visible again.

### A savings tracker with no streak to break

Same philosophy as the original planner's habit tracker: thirty circles, fill one in whenever money gets set aside, big or small. No deadline, no reset-to-zero shame for a slow month.

### Everything else a real budget needs

Weekly spending, bill payments, debt payoff, spending by category, and a net worth snapshot round out the system — the boring parts of managing money, written down in one place instead of scattered across apps you stopped opening.
